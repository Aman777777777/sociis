Only what is needed.

This site supports optional Google Analytics, which loads only after you accept it when configured. No advertising trackers are used.

What our website code does

Before you accept analytics, the site does not load Google Analytics or set its cookies. Your accept or reject choice is saved in this browser for up to six months. If accepted, Google Analytics uses first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_<container-id> to distinguish visitors and sessions.

Fonts, photographs and films are served with the website. We do not embed social media feeds, advertising pixels or external video players.

Google Analytics

If the analytics tag is configured and you accept, page views and related browser/device information are sent to Google Analytics for audience measurement. Google explains its data handling for EU visitors in its EU privacy information ↗. We do not use Analytics for advertising.

Hosting and other services

The hosting infrastructure may use technical mechanisms needed for delivery or security. This notice does not claim that every service used by your browser is free of cookies. Information about the hosting provider is included in our Privacy notice.

If you follow an external link, use LinkedIn or Sociis House, or open your email application, that service has its own privacy and cookie arrangements.

Your choice

Analytics is optional. Accept and Reject analytics are offered with equal access to the site; scrolling does not count as consent. You can reject or withdraw your choice using Cookie settings. Analytics is disabled again when you reject it.
