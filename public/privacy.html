Visiting the website

This site supports optional Google Analytics. When configured, it loads only if you choose “Accept analytics”. If enabled, it records page views and related browser/device information to measure use of the site. Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to distinguish visitors and sessions. Google describes its handling of IP addresses in its EU privacy information ↗. We do not use analytics for advertising or intentionally send form contents or other personal details to Google Analytics. Rejecting analytics does not affect access to the site.

Your choice is saved in this browser for up to six months. You can change it using Cookie settings on the site. Photographs, films and fonts are served with the website. The website host may process technical request information, such as an IP address, browser details, time and requested resource, to deliver and secure the site.

Starting a conversation

Your topic and message remain in the page while you compose them. Choosing Open email draft passes them to your email application. This website does not submit the message to sociisgroup. We receive it only if you send the email.

If you contact us, we process the information you provide, such as your name, email address, organisation and enquiry, to respond and manage the conversation. We rely on steps requested before a potential contract where applicable, or our legitimate interest in answering enquiries. We do not add you to a marketing list through this contact interaction.

Access, providers and retention

Information is accessible to people handling your enquiry and the hosting and email providers needed to deliver those services. We may retain records required by law or reasonably needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We keep enquiry records while the conversation remains active and for relevant follow up. Once that purpose ends, we delete or anonymise records unless a legal obligation or justified need requires retention. Technical hosting records follow the provider’s applicable retention arrangements. Contact us for details relevant to your enquiry.

Our service providers may process information outside the European Economic Area under their applicable transfer arrangements. You can ask us about processing locations and safeguards relevant to your information.

Your rights

Depending on the processing and applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Contact hello@sociisgroup.com. We respond without undue delay and normally within one month; where the GDPR allows an extension, we will explain it within that period. You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority ↗.

Scope and changes

This notice covers the website and initial enquiries. BEE is in development; its future product use will require its own privacy information. We will update this notice when the website’s processing changes.
